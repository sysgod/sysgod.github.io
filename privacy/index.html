Privacy Policy

This single policy applies to all mobile games (the “Apps”) published by Axis Games, and explains what data they access, collect, use and share, how long it is kept, and how you can ask us to delete it. By using any App you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Apps.

1. Who we are

The Apps are published by VrAxis Doo (Axis Games) (“we”, “us”, “Axis Games”). Contact: axisgamedev@gmail.com.

3. How we use data

  • To operate the Apps and save your progress;
  • to show ads and let you watch rewarded videos;
  • to process in-app purchases and restore them;
  • to understand how the Apps are used, fix bugs and improve game balance;
  • to detect and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We share data only with service providers that process it for the purposes above. They have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play Billing – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity LevelPlay / ironSource (ad mediation) – is.com/privacy-policy
  • AppLovin MAX (ad mediation) – applovin.com/privacy
  • Ad networks reached through the mediation above, which may include Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Pangle (ByteDance), Vungle/Liftoff, Chartboost, InMobi, DT Exchange (Fyber), Moloco, BidMachine, Bigo Ads, Verve, Yandex and others; each network's privacy policy applies to its ads
  • Adjust (install attribution and analytics) – adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • GameAnalytics (game analytics) – gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Unity Analytics and Unity In-App Purchasing – unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

5. Advertising choices

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states) the Apps ask for your consent before personalized ads and you can change it in each game’s settings: Settings → Privacy.
  • Buying No Ads (if offered) removes banner and interstitial ads.

6. Children

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data sent from the Apps to our partners is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) where the partner supports it. Game saves are stored in each game’s private storage on your device. No method of storage or transmission is 100 % secure.

8. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: game data is removed when you uninstall an App or clear its storage.
  • Held by us or our providers: analytics and advertising data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes above (typically up to 26 months), then deleted or anonymized.
  • Deletion request: since our Apps have no accounts, we usually cannot identify you from the data. If you want data linked to your advertising ID deleted, e-mail axisgamedev@gmail.com with your advertising ID (Settings → Google → Ads) and we will act on it or forward it to our providers within 30 days.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EEA/UK, CCPA/CPRA in California, LGPD in Brazil) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact axisgamedev@gmail.com.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States and the EU. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the current version; material changes will be announced in the Apps or on this page.
